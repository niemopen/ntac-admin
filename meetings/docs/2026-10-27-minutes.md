## New business

* SSGT security vulnerability

  - The group focused on a critical Apache Struts vulnerability affecting SSGT
  - Three paths were discussed:
    - remediate the vulnerability
    - migrate SSGT to AWS
    - decommission SSGT until the toolbox and API 2.0 are available
  - The team wanted more detail on cost and effort

* GTRI follow-up meeting

  - Jim Cabral agreed to lead a meeting with GTRI on SSGT migration
  - Key questions include:
    - how GTRI arrived at its engineering estimates
    - how SSGT usage numbers were calculated
    - what impact each option would have on the toolbox timeline
    - when API 2.0 and NIEM Toolbox will be ready to replace SSGT
  - Make sure DHS is in the loop for this discussion

* Token policy update

  - The team briefly noted that Brian Boyd's group is developing token use guidelines
  - Brian will attend the initial meeting of that group next week

* RDF 1.2 and NIEM specs

  - NDR 6 uses RDF-star for relationship properties, and RDF-star is incorporated into RDF 1.2
  - So it appears likely that NIEM will adopt RDF 1.2

* Naming and design rules issue

  - Scott raised a [new NDR issue](https://github.com/niemopen/niem-naming-design-rules/issues/51) involving wildcards in the naming and design rules
  - He proposed making assumptions about wildcard placement explicit
  - This was presented as a simpler alternative to more complex metamodel changes
  - Discuss and perhaps approve at next meeting
