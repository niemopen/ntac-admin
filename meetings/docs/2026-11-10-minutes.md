## New business

* SSGT follow-up with GTRI

  - Jim reported that the follow-up conversation with GTRI took place
  - Key GTRI IT support staff were not present, so important questions remain unanswered
  - Outstanding questions include:
    - whether compensating controls are possible for the SSGT security vulnerability
    - what shutdown date GTRI or its IT staff would require if the system cannot be remediated
    - what the actual SSGT usage numbers are, including active users and subset downloads
  - The group agreed that better usage data is needed before making a final decision

* NIEM tooling support and SSGT scope

  - The group discussed how SSGT relates to current and future NIEM tooling support
  - CMFTool supports NIEM 3 and above
  - Support for NIEM 1 and 2 is possible but very low priority for the developer
  - The group generally agreed that NIEM 6 tooling should be prioritized over preserving support for the oldest versions

* Token policy update

  - Brian shared that he has drafted an initial token use policy
  - Jim and Eric reviewed the draft and provided feedback
  - The current direction is to keep the policy flexible across providers and models
  - The group preferred relying on existing provider tooling rather than creating a custom token management system
  - Brian will continue refining the draft before broader NTAC review

* FBI CJIS briefing and domain funding

  - There will be a briefing for FBI CJIS
  - The goal is to educate CJIS on NIEM 6.0
  - The discussion will also explore possible funding for the forensics and financial integrity domains

* Financial Integrity Working Group update

  - Bradley Bolliger noted that new co-chairs have been appointed for the Financial Integrity Working Group
  - Natalie Lobner and Carol House will serve in those roles
  - The group is making progress on a data specification for financial crime and fraud
  - That work may begin affecting operations in $Q1$ or $Q2$ of 2027

* NDR 6.1 wildcard issue

  - The group revisited Scott's proposal to make assumptions about wildcard usage explicit in the rules
  - This would likely be handled by adding one or two new rules to NDR 6.1
  - Jim supported the proposed direction
  - The group reached a lazy consensus to proceed with that update
