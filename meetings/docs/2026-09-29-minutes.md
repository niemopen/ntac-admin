## New business

* CMF tool and roadmap

  - NLETS attendees showed interest in NIEM 6 and CMF
  - Main concern was the lack of a replacement for SSGT
  - The group agreed a clearer tool roadmap and statement of work are needed
  - Brad Bolliger will draft a roadmap and help re-engage GTRI

* Version 6.0 priorities

  - Paul Wormeli emphasized getting the existing tool set working with version 6.0
  - Subset generation remains a critical gap
  - The team agreed to stabilize current tools before expanding scope

* Ontology Mapper and ontology support

  - Attendees were interested in expanding the Ontology Mapper
  - Ideas included multiple ontologies, database connections, and CMF RDF output
  - The group discussed possible support for NIEM, NODS, and SALI
  - They agreed to keep evaluating multi-ontology support while focusing first on core priorities

* OKN and token access

  - The Ontology Mapper was discussed in the context of OKN and broader ontology interoperability
  - Brian Boyd raised concerns about token costs for development
  - Possible solutions included pooled access, shared accounts, or support through OASIS
  - Jim Cabral will explore whether OASIS can help obtain free model access

* Communications and outreach

  - Paul announced PGB approval of a new communications and outreach committee
  - Attendees were asked to send Paul names of possible candidates
